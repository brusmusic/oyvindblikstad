What it is

A field of sound journeys that starts where you are.

Resonance Atlas is being built as a simple phone-based entrance into ReSonance Lab's tuning work. You choose a room, listen, and let the journey move through low-frequency relationships designed in Tuner Composer.

The first beta is intentionally small: a visual atlas, a set of guided sound states, and a way to find a personal starting frequency before the journey begins.

Current beta Mobile-first sound journeys

Best tested with headphones or a connected resonance mat. Access is limited while the system is tuned.
